Our Story

How a simple idea grew into a movement for authentic connection

The Beginning of Something Meaningful

UPTOPIA was born in 2018 from a shared frustration with superficial social interactions in the digital age. Our founders, Sarah and Michael, noticed how technology was connecting people globally but isolating them locally. Despite living in a bustling city, they struggled to form meaningful connections with neighbors and people who shared their interests.

After hosting a small dinner party that brought together diverse but like-minded individuals, they realized the transformative power of shared experiences. That single dinner sparked friendships, collaborations, and a sense of community that lasted far beyond the event.

We started with a simple mission: create a platform where passionate hosts could share their skills and interests with community-minded attendees. What began as a small project among friends in San Francisco has grown into a global community with thousands of experiences across 50 cities.
